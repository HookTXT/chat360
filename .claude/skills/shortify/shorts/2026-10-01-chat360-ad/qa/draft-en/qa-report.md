**What I reviewed:** `build/draft-en.mp4` (rendered 17:57), all 8 contact sheets, about 40 full-size frames (at least one per scene), and exact frames extracted at 30 fps around every cut and anything that looked off. I also checked phone legibility by viewing frames at 390 px wide. I did not redo the automatic checks (length, loudness, cuts, tone split); see `auto.md`.

## What works

- **Hook:** one huge, bold line you can read in under a second. It holds for 4 s with a slow push-in, stays dark, and works as a cover.
- **Numbers:** the "4+ hours" and "34%" frames are big, centered, clean and sourced.
- **Logo:** correct on both backgrounds (as supplied on paper, "360" reversed to white on dark). The mint DEMO pill uses dark ink, as the style sheet requires.
- **Chat widget:** it looks like a real, polished product (header, online status, vehicle card, "Test drive booked" confirmation), which is believable for a dealer.
- **Twist headline:** the struck, dimmed "Other chats send a link." against the mint "opens the page." is the clearest idea in the ad.
- **List:** rows build one per beat, with the previous row dimmed. The "It speaks first" visual (Civic page, greeting, quick replies) reads instantly.
- **Bilingual:** the bubbles are large, and the Québec "su'l" is a good touch for "truly bilingual".
- **CTA:** "Comment DEMO", the typed comment box and the send button are clear and well sized, and the ad ends dark.
- **Safe zones:** text stays out of all safe zones for the whole ad.
