## Pick: #1

- Frame 0 shows it as the visitor's message in a dealership-website chat, on a vehicle page, at night (no clock time — `topic.md` gives none). Without the chat bubble it reads like a ChatGPT prompt.
- No number in the hook.
- FR keeps tutoiement (a casual challenge); "auto" over "char" — the ad speaks to dealer staff, not a meme crowd.
- The rest of the short answers the challenge: the AI pitches the car (site's own HR-V pitch) and books the test drive.
