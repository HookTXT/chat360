# Dissect a script into visual moments (plan subagent)

Inputs: locked script `[SHORT FOLDER]/script.md`, word timestamps from `[SHORT FOLDER]/voice/`.

Split every sentence into small visual moments. For each: start/end time, job (hook, problem, claim, number, list item, twist, payoff, CTA), drawable nouns, and the verb. The verb picks the motion (see `../GRAPHICS.md`). Then choose: the real thing, a library graphic (by ID), or Kevin's face.

Rules: the hook has no cuts; anything under 0.5 s is a step on the current graphic; claims show proof within 2 s; screenshots must read in 1 s at phone size or become our own graphic; dark/light runs per `../STYLE.md`. Flag any moment no graphic can draw.

Save the table to `[SHORT FOLDER]/plan.md` and show it before building.
