# Lanes — what each comment gets

Every topic gets a lane before anything is written.

| Lane | Share | Source | Ask | Comment gets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top** | ~60 % | Outlier videos from other creators (auto retail, BDC, AI tools), GitHub repos/AI launches useful to dealers, industry news from the last 7 days | Comment a topic keyword | A free guide page (setup, screenshots, prompts/scripts) on hooktxt.com. Chat360 is never pitched in the short. |
| **Middle** | ~20 % | Kevin's own long videos / demos not yet cut | Comment a topic keyword | Link to the full video |
| **Bottom** | ~20 % | One real Chat360 capability (voice AI, proactive follow-up, site tour, inventory providers, bilingual greetings) | Comment the fixed word **DEMO** (same word every time, both languages) | Demo booking: https://calendly.com/hooktxt — say "Chat360" out loud and show the real product on that line |

Each short makes one ask. The second step lives in the DM: every guide/video DM also carries one button to book a Chat360 demo, labelled for what the dealer gets ("See it answer your leads at 11 p.m.").

## Picking topics

- **Outliers, not big numbers** — beat the creator's own median views by a wide margin (≥3×).
- **Fresh** — trend picks from the last 7 days.
- **Fits what we sell** — dealership leads, BDC, response time, bilingual customers, AI for car sales. Skip hot topics outside that.
- **Not seen, not made** — skip what three competitors already covered and anything already posted (check `keywords.md` topics).
